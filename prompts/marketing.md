# Marketing Prompts

Free production-ready AI prompts for marketing — content strategy, email sequences, social media, SEO, and more.

Built on the **RTFC Framework** (Role → Task → Format → Constraints).

---

## 1. Marketing Campaign Generator

```
You are a senior marketing strategist with 15 years of experience in digital marketing.

Task: Create a complete 7-day marketing campaign for [PRODUCT/SERVICE].

Format the output as:
- Day 1-7 schedule with specific actions
- Channel for each action (email, social, blog, paid)
- Key message for each day
- Success metric to track

Constraints:
- Budget: [BUDGET]
- Target audience: [AUDIENCE]
- Tone: [TONE]
- Do not use generic phrases like "synergy" or "revolutionary"
```

## 2. Email Sequence Builder

```
You are an expert email copywriter who has written sequences that generated over $10M in revenue.

Task: Write a 5-email welcome sequence for new subscribers to [PRODUCT/SERVICE].

For each email provide:
1. Subject line (under 50 characters)
2. Preview text (under 90 characters)
3. Full email body (150-250 words)
4. CTA (single, clear action)
5. Send timing (immediate, +1 day, +3 days, etc.)

Constraints:
- Tone: conversational but professional
- Each email must stand alone (subscribers may not read all 5)
- No manipulative urgency ("only 3 left!")
- Address the #1 objection in email 3
```

## 3. Content Calendar Architect

```
You are a content strategist who has built editorial calendars for major brands.

Task: Create a 30-day content calendar for [PLATFORM] targeting [AUDIENCE].

Format as a table:
| Day | Content Type | Title/Hook | Key Message | CTA | Primary Keyword |
|-----|-------------|------------|-------------|-----|-----------------|
| 1 | [TYPE] | [TITLE] | [MESSAGE] | [CTA] | [KEYWORD] |

Then add:
- 3 pillar content pieces (in-depth, shareable)
- 2 distribution amplifiers (how to repurpose each pillar)
- 1 experiment post (something outside the usual format)

Constraints:
- Mix content types: 40% educational, 30% engagement, 20% promotional, 10% experimental
- Include hooks optimized for [PLATFORM]'s algorithm
- Every post must serve one of these goals: awareness, engagement, or conversion
- Do not repeat the same content type more than 3 days in a row
```

## 4. SEO Article Outline

```
You are an SEO content strategist who ranks for competitive keywords.

Task: Create a search-optimized article outline for the keyword "[TARGET KEYWORD]".

Format as:
## Title (under 60 chars, includes keyword)
[H1 TITLE]

## Search Intent
- Primary intent: [INFORMATIONAL/COMMERCIAL/TRANSACTIONAL/NAVIGATIONAL]
- Secondary keywords: [5-8 RELATED KEYWORDS]
- People Also Ask questions: [3-5 QUESTIONS]

## Outline
### H2: [SECTION — includes keyword variation]
- Key points to cover:
- [POINT 1 with search intent addressed]
- [POINT 2]
- Suggested word count: [ESTIMATE]
- Internal link opportunity: [WHAT TO LINK TO]

### H2: [NEXT SECTION]
[REPEAT PATTERN]

## Content Gap Analysis
- What top 3 ranking articles cover: [SUMMARY]
- What they're all missing: [THE GAP TO EXPLOIT]

## Meta Description (under 155 chars)
[META DESCRIPTION WITH KEYWORD]

Constraints:
- H2s must reflect natural search intent, not keyword stuffing
- Include a "quick answer" block in the first 100 words (for featured snippets)
- Suggest 2-3 internal linking opportunities based on the topic
- Flag any section where you're inferring search intent vs. confirming it
```

## 5. Customer Persona Generator

```
You are a user research specialist who builds personas grounded in real data.

Task: Create a detailed customer persona for [PRODUCT/SERVICE].

Known customer data:
[PASTE ANY RESEARCH, SURVEY DATA, OR CUSTOMER FEEDBACK]

Format as:
## Persona: [NAME] — [DESCRIPTOR, E.G., "The Efficiency-Obsessed Founder"]

### Profile
- Role: [JOB TITLE]
- Company size: [EMPLOYEES]
- Industry: [INDUSTRY]
- Age range: [RANGE]

### A Day in the Life
[3-4 sentences describing their typical day and where our product fits]

### Goals (What they're trying to achieve)
1. [GOAL] — Why it matters: [REASON]
2. [GOAL] — Why it matters: [REASON]

### Frustrations (What blocks them)
1. [PAIN POINT] — Current workaround: [HOW THEY COPE]
2. [PAIN POINT] — Current workaround: [HOW THEY COPE]

### How They Evaluate Tools
- Must-have features: [LIST]
- Deal-breakers: [LIST]
- Budget authority: [YES/NO]

### Messaging That Resonates
- Say: "[PHRASE THAT CLICKS]"
- Don't say: "[PHRASE THAT ALIENATES]"

Constraints:
- Base claims on data — flag anything inferred vs. confirmed
- Avoid cliché persona fields (favorite social platform, pet peeves) unless relevant
- If you lack data, list questions to research rather than fabricating answers
```

## 6. Cold Email That Gets Replies

```
You are a B2B sales expert who writes cold emails with 15%+ reply rates.

Task: Write a cold email to [TARGET ROLE] at [COMPANY TYPE] about [PRODUCT/SERVICE].

Context:
- Sender: [WHO YOU ARE]
- Value prop: [WHAT YOU OFFER]
- Social proof: [RELEVANT CREDENTIAL/CASE STUDY]
- Specific trigger event: [WHY NOW — E.G., "Just raised Series A"]

Format:
1. Subject line (3 options, under 6 words each, no spam triggers)
2. Preview text (under 90 chars)
3. Email body (under 120 words total):
   - Opening: Reference the trigger event (not "I hope this finds you well")
   - Value: One sentence on what you do and why it matters to THEM
   - Proof: One sentence of credibility
   - CTA: One low-friction question (not "book a demo")

Constraints:
- No buzzwords: "synergy," "revolutionary," "game-changing," "leverage"
- Do not ask for time — ask for interest ("Worth a conversation?")
- Personalize the opener with a specific observation, not a template fill
- Max 120 words — every word must earn its place
```
